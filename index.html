About the Game

You arrive back to a familiar island filled with white sands, clear oceans, and salty winds. It is Destiny Island, your home. However, you stumbled upon another clone of yourself? You must prove that you are the real Sora by beating the "imposter" up! Who will reign supreme? Which power is stronger? Is light the heart's true nature, or is the other way around?

Coast Pyter is a simple 2D fighting game where two players control their own Sora with access to a limited number of moves in order to fight each other until one's HP bar runs out. Be careful! You only get a small amount of HP so make each hit count!

The mission of the game is to teach players this fundamental fighting game concept called "Footsies" where players must control the space to gain the upper hand. Often times this inolves careful movement, poking, whiff punishing, and hit confirming.

This game is coded in Python and accessing the Python module to access its game dev library. Although the assets of the game is heavily inspired by a JRPG "Kingdom Hearts", the main inspiration of the game came from an indie fighting game called "Footsies", where you only gain access to a small number of attacks, 3 HP bars, and only a 2 hit combo. Like Coast Pyter, the mission of the game is to teach players the concept of "Footsies" in a simplified scenario.

Tips

  • What and when to attack?
    Light Attacks are fast and reliable, especially in close range, use this to check your opponent.
    Standing Light Attack is the most accesible and reliable while Crouching Light Attack has slighly lower range but hits low.
    Heavy attacks are long ranged and high damage, use them at long ranges to avoid getting punished.
    Standing Heavy Attack has good damage and range while Crouching Heavy Attack has more damage but recovers slower.
  • Spacing!
    Controlling the space is crucial. Proper spacing allows you to land hits, safely poke, and punish opponent whiffs.
  • Annnnd you're blocked
    A good defense is a good offense. Block attacks to avoid getting hit and wait for the opponent to make a mistake
    Standing Block negates all Standing Attacks but not Crouching Attacks.
    Crouching Block negates almost all Attacks except for Standing Heavy.
  • Mix em up!
    Mix up your offense by constantly switching between Standing and Crouching attacks. This would leave your opponent guessing and soon enough they will slip up.
  • Combos
    You can do combos to dish out more damage. Hit them with any light attack and follow it up with a heavy attack.
    (Keep in mind that combos only works when you confirmed that your Light Attack hits an enemy and is not blocked.)
